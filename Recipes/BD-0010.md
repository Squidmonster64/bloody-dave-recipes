## Ingredients

### Buy

- 2 cloves Garlic
- 1 stalk Celery
- 0.5 Brown Onion
- 1 packet Ginger Paste
- 1 packet Beef Strips
- 1 bag Slaw Mix
- 1 sachet Mixed Sesame Seeds
- 6 Mini Flour Tortillas
- 1 packet Garlic Aioli
- 1 packet Japanese Dressing
- 1 Cucumber

### Pantry

- 1 drizzle Olive Oil
- 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
- 1 tbsp Honey

## Method

1. Finely chop garlic and celery. Thinly slice onion. In a small bowl, combine garlic, ginger paste, the soy sauce and honey.
2. In a large frying pan, heat a drizzle of olive oil over medium-high heat. Cook onion until tender, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a bowl. Return pan to high heat with a drizzle of olive oil. When oil is hot, cook beef strips, in batches (to help it stay tender), until browned and cooked through, 1-2 minutes. Return beef strips and onion to pan. Add honey-soy mixture and simmer until heated through, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
3. While ingredients are simmering, combine Japanese dressing and a drizzle of olive oil in a medium bowl. Add slaw mix, celery and 1/2 the mixed sesame seeds. Toss to coat. Set aside. Microwave mini flour tortillas on a plate in 10 second bursts, until heated through.
4. Spread each tortilla with garlic aioli. Fill with a helping of slaw, Japanese-style beef and cucumber. Sprinkle with remaining sesame seeds to serve.
