## Ingredients

### Buy

- 1 Capsicum
- 1 packet Passata
- 2 cloves Garlic
- 1 Spring Onion
- 1 sachet Chilli Flakes
- 1 tin Sweetcorn
- 1 packet Cheddar Cheese
- 1 packet Gnocchi
- 1 sachet Mexican Fiesta Spice Blend
- 1 Carrot

### Pantry

- 1 drizzle Olive Oil
- 1 splash Water
- 1 pinch Brown Sugar
- 1 knob Butter

## Method

1. Grate carrot. Thinly slice spring onion. Roughly chop capsicum. Drain sweetcorn.
2. In a large frying pan, heat a drizzle of olive oil over high heat. Cook capsicum, corn and carrot, tossing, until lightly browned, 4-6 minutes. Transfer to a bowl.
3. Wipe out frying pan, then return to medium-high heat with a generous drizzle of olive oil. When oil is hot, cook gnocchi, tossing occasionally, until golden, 6-8 minutes.
4. Preheat grill to medium-high. To pan with the gnocchi, add Mexican Fiesta spice blend and garlic paste. Cook, tossing, until fragrant, 1 minute. Return cooked veggies to the pan, then add the water, the brown sugar, the butter and passata. Stir to combine, then simmer until slightly thickened, 1-2 minutes. Season to taste.
5. Transfer the saucy gnocchi to a baking dish. Sprinkle with Cheddar cheese. Grill until cheese is melted and golden, 5-10 minutes.
6. Divide cheesy Mexican-spiced grilled gnocchi between bowls. Top with chilli flakes (if using) and sprinkle over spring onions to serve.
