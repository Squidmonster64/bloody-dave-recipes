## Ingredients

### Buy

- 1 Carrot
- 1 Sweet Potato
- 1 Beetroot
- 1 packet Beef Rump Steak
- 1 bag Baby Spinach Leaves
- 1 packet Mustard Mayo

### Pantry

- 1 drizzle Olive Oil
- 1 tsp White Wine Vinegar
- 1 pinch Salt
- 1 pinch Pepper

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 240°C/220°C fan-forced. Thickly slice carrot into half-moons. Cut sweet potato into bite-sized chunks. Cut beetroot into 1cm chunks.
2. Place prepped veggies on a lined oven tray. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper and toss to coat. Roast until tender, 20-25 minutes. When the veggies have 8 minutes remaining, add baby spinach and a pinch of salt to the tray. Gently toss to combine. Roast until tender, a further 5-8 minutes.
3. Place beef rump between two sheets of baking paper. Pound beef with a meat mallet or rolling pin until slightly flattened. Season beef with salt and pepper.
4. In a large frying pan, heat a drizzle of olive oil over high heat. When oil is hot, cook the beef, turning, for 3-6 minutes (depending on thickness), or until cooked to your liking. Transfer to a plate to rest.
5. While the steak is resting, add the white wine vinegar to the roast veggie tray. Gently toss to combine.
6. Slice the seared steak. Divide steak and roast veggie toss between plates. Spoon any resting juices over steak. Top with a dollop of mustard mayo to serve.
